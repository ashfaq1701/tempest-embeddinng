================================================================================
Is our tangent-space head valid geometric DL? Defenders, critics, and the verdict
================================================================================

Context / our architecture
--------------------------
Learned node embeddings E on the unit SPHERE (geoopt.Sphere ManifoldParameter),
optimized by RiemannianAdam. Per query:
  1. Log_{E[u]}(E[token]) maps neighbour points into the TANGENT SPACE at E[u].
  2. STANDARD EUCLIDEAN layers run on those tangent vectors — attention, a Linear
     stem, and a stack of pre-norm residual FFN blocks (LayerNorm -> Linear ->
     GELU -> Linear -> Dropout, per-channel LayerScale) — producing a displaced
     tangent vector mu.
  3. mu is projected ONCE back onto the tangent space and mapped to the manifold
     with a SINGLE exp map, P[u] = exp_{E[u]}(mu).
  4. Links scored by GEODESIC DISTANCE d(P[u], E[v]) = arccos<P[u],E[v]> -> MLP.
Key properties: exactly ONE logmap + ONE expmap per forward (no repeated
round-trips); the tangent-space ops (LayerNorm, dense MLP) are COORDINATE-
DEPENDENT, i.e. NOT rotation-equivariant / not intrinsic.

Question: by which leading geometric-DL papers' standards is this legitimate, and
is non-rotation-equivariance in the tangent space a real problem or normal?


================================================================================
EXECUTIVE VERDICT: legitimate, mainstream, and at the CLEANEST end
================================================================================
Every load-bearing component of the design has a NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR precedent that
does the same thing and treats it as standard. The critics (HyboNet, H2H-GCN)
target a DIFFERENT regime — REPEATED per-layer round-trips — and their own text
("especially FREQUENTLY using tangent space approximations") does not indict a
single projection. Non-rotation-equivariance of tangent-space ops is NORMAL
practice in the embedding literature, not a disqualifier; coordinate-independence
is a goal pursued by a separate sub-field (gauge / spherical CNNs on signal
fields), not a standard applied to embedding models like ours.


================================================================================
1 & 2. DEFENDERS (ranked) — the "log -> Euclidean op -> exp" recipe
================================================================================

#1  HGCN — Chami, Ying, Re, Leskovec, NeurIPS 2019, arXiv:1910.12933.
    Closest match: the recipe AND a PER-NODE base point (log/exp at the center
    node's own embedding, exactly like our E[u]) AND a geodesic-distance decoder.
    - Feature transform (Eq. 6): W (x)^K x^H := exp_o^K(W log_o^K(x^H)); "the
      matrix representing the transform is defined on the tangent space, which is
      Euclidean and isomorphic to R^d."
    - Aggregation at the node's own point (Eq. 9):
      Agg^K(x^H)_i = exp_{x_i^H}^K( Sum_j w_ij log_{x_i^H}^K(x_j^H) ) — "aggregation
      is directly performed in the tangent space of each center point x_i^H, AS
      THIS IS WHERE THE EUCLIDEAN APPROXIMATION IS BEST." Ablation: beats
      aggregation at the origin.
    - Coordinate-dependent op: sigma^(x)(x^H) = exp_o(sigma(log_o(x^H))) (Eq. 10).
    => Our "log/exp at E[u]" is HGCN's RECOMMENDED construction, not a liberty.

#2  Hyperbolic Neural Networks — Ganea, Becigneul, Hofmann, NeurIPS 2018,
    arXiv:1805.09112. Canonical origin of the recipe.
    - Def. 3.2: f^(x)c(x) := exp_0^c( f( log_0^c(x) ) ).
    - COLLAPSE THEOREM (blesses our design): a stack of tangent layers collapses
      to a SINGLE round-trip: f_k^(x) o ... o f_1^(x) = exp_0 o f_k o ... o f_1 o log_0.
      Our one-log/one-exp head IS the algebraic ideal a deep tangent stack only
      approximates.
    - T_0 D = R^n (tangent space is the Euclidean vector space).

#3  kappa-GCN (Constant Curvature GCN) — Bachmann, Becigneul, Ganea, ICML 2020,
    arXiv:1911.05076. PRIMARY SPHERE DEFENDER: same recipe explicitly extended to
    POSITIVE curvature.
    - kappa-Right-Matrix-Mult (Eq. 10): (X (x)k W)_i = exp_0^k((log_0^k(X) W)_i);
      tangent aggregation exp_x^k(Sum_i alpha_i log_x^k(x_i)).
    - "For positive curvature, we choose the stereographic projection of the
      sphere..."; models "smoothly recover their Euclidean counterparts when the
      curvature goes to zero FROM EITHER SIDE."

#4  Hyperbolic Graph Neural Networks — Liu, Nickel, Kiela, NeurIPS 2019,
    arXiv:1910.12892. Manifold-agnostic recipe:
    h_u^{k+1} = sigma( exp_{x'}( Sum_v A~_uv W^k log_{x'}(h_v^k) ) ).

#5  HNN++ — Shimizu, Mukuta, Harada, ICLR 2021, arXiv:2006.08210. Concatenation,
    split, and attention done in the tangent space then exp back (closed-form
    generalization of Ganea).

#6  LAYERNORM SMOKING GUN — HVT, "A Comprehensive Vision Framework for Learning in
    Non-Euclidean Space", arXiv:2409.16897. Publishes our exact coordinate-
    dependent ops:
      LayerNorm^D(x) = exp_0( LayerNorm( log_0(x) ) )
      ReLU^D(x)      = exp_0( ReLU( log_0(x) ) )
    "Activation functions and normalization are applied in the tangent space at
    the origin to leverage familiar Euclidean operations."

"Tangent space is a Euclidean vector space where standard ops apply" (the exact
framing): HGNN review arXiv:2202.13852 — "the tangent space T_x M is an
n-dimension vector space and isomorphic to R^n... the transformation takes place
there. Subsequently, the nodes are mapped back... using the exponential map."

ADVERSARIAL CORRECTION (do NOT miscite as a defender): Gulcehre et al.,
Hyperbolic Attention Networks (ICLR 2019, arXiv:1805.09786) is NOT a
tangent-space-recipe paper — it uses INTRINSIC ops (attention by hyperbolic
distance, aggregation by the Einstein midpoint in the Klein model, no log/exp
sandwich). It belongs on the intrinsic/critic side.

Which defenders use coordinate-dependent / non-rotation-equivariant tangent ops:
HVT (LayerNorm + ReLU), Ganea (pointwise nonlinearity), HGCN (nonlinearity),
kappa-GCN (matrix multiply), HNN++ (concat/attention) — ALL standard, NONE treats
coordinate-dependence as a problem. No defender explicitly concedes-and-defends
the equivariance loss; it simply is never raised (which is the evidence it is
considered normal).


================================================================================
3. GEODESIC-DISTANCE DECODERS are standard
================================================================================
- HGCN (arXiv:1910.12933) — Fermi-Dirac decoder, exact match to our form:
  p((i,j) in E | x_i,x_j) = [ exp((d(x_i,x_j)^2 - r)/t) + 1 ]^-1, d = geodesic.
- Nickel & Kiela, Poincare Embeddings (NeurIPS 2017, arXiv:1705.08039) — softmax
  over negative geodesic distance: L = Sum log( e^{-d(u,v)} / Sum_{v'} e^{-d(u,v')} ).
- Ganea, Hyperbolic Entailment Cones (ICML 2018, arXiv:1804.01882) — angular
  (geodesic-cone) energy.
- SPHERE-SPECIFIC: Mettes et al., Hyperspherical Prototype Networks (NeurIPS 2019,
  arXiv:1901.10514) — classify by argmax cos theta (= arccos<.,.> on S^{D-1}),
  prototypes on the unit sphere. OUR EXACT DECODER ON OUR EXACT MANIFOLD.
- Davidson et al., Hyperspherical VAE (UAI 2018, arXiv:1804.00891) — vMF on
  S^{m-1}, similarity via mu^T z (cosine/geodesic).


================================================================================
3b. SPHERE / POSITIVE-CURVATURE defenders
================================================================================
- kappa-GCN (arXiv:1911.05076) — tangent-space recipe for spheres. Primary.
- Gu, Sala, Re et al., Learning Mixed-Curvature Representations in Product Spaces
  (ICLR 2019, OpenReview HJxeWnCcF7) — product manifolds with explicit SPHERICAL
  factors, Riemannian optimization.
- Skopek, Ganea, Becigneul, Mixed-curvature VAEs (ICLR 2020, arXiv:1911.08411) —
  VAE on spherical latents S_K^n with sphere exp/log; uses PARALLEL TRANSPORT
  between tangent spaces at different base points (the principled tool for
  per-point tangent spaces like ours).
- Becigneul & Ganea, Riemannian Adaptive Optimization / RiemannianAdam
  (ICLR 2019, arXiv:1810.00760) — the optimizer we use (sphere qualifies;
  framework-level, headline experiment is hyperbolic).
- geoopt (arXiv:2005.02819) — geoopt.Sphere + RiemannianAdam is our toolchain,
  documented as standard.


================================================================================
4. CRITICS (strongest quotes)
================================================================================
- Chen et al., Fully Hyperbolic NN / HyboNet, ACL 2022, arXiv:2105.14686.
  - Expressiveness: "linear transformation in tangent spaces used by existing
    hyperbolic networks is A RELAXATION OF THE LORENTZ ROTATION AND DOES NOT
    INCLUDE THE BOOST, implicitly limiting the capabilities."
  - Stability of repeated maps: "transform features between hyperbolic spaces and
    tangent spaces via the logarithmic and exponential maps... The compositions of
    these functions are complicated and usually range to infinity, significantly
    weakening the stability."
- Dai et al., H2H-GCN, CVPR 2021, arXiv:2104.06942.
  - "tangent space is only a local approximation of a manifold"; the mapping "is
    only locally diffeomorphic, which may distort the global structure...
    ESPECIALLY FREQUENTLY USING TANGENT SPACE APPROXIMATIONS." Targets the
    per-layer manifold-tangent-manifold scheme.
- Hypformer (KDD 2024, arXiv:2407.01290) and the Hyperbolic DNN survey
  (arXiv:2101.04562) echo: tangent methods "can lead to significant mapping errors
  for distant points"; stacking tangent-space CNNs "may collapse to a vanilla
  Euclidean CNN."


================================================================================
5. Does the critique bite OUR design? NO.
================================================================================
(a) Expressiveness (HyboNet): a tangent-space LINEAR map is weaker than a full
    Lorentz transformation (missing the boost). Narrow: (i) hyperbolic-specific,
    no sphere claim; (ii) we do NOT use a bare linear map — we use a full
    nonlinear Euclidean net (attention + residual FFNs), so "linear map lacks a
    boost" is largely orthogonal to what we compute.
(b) Round-trip distortion (H2H-GCN): its own wording is about FREQUENCY —
    "especially frequently using tangent space approximations," aimed at the
    per-layer scheme. We do EXACTLY ONE log + ONE exp per forward — no repeated
    round-trip to accumulate distortion. Ganea's collapse theorem
    (exp_0 o f_k...f_1 o log_0) says a single-projection network is the IDEAL form
    a deep tangent stack can only approximate. We are already at the ideal.
Base-point choice: log/exp at the node's own E[u] (not origin) is HGCN's
    recommended, empirically superior construction; parallel transport (Skopek)
    is the standard machinery for per-point tangent spaces.
Non-rotation-equivariance: NORMAL across the entire embedding literature (Ganea,
    HGCN, HNN++, kappa-GCN, HVT, Mathieu Poincare VAE arXiv:1901.06033, Skopek,
    Davidson). The "coordinate-dependence is a flaw" position exists only in the
    gauge / spherical-CNN line (Cohen et al., arXiv:1902.04615, 1801.10130), which
    solves a DIFFERENT problem (equivariant convolution of signal fields over a
    manifold) and is not a standard applied to embedding models. So
    non-rotation-equivariance is NOT disqualifying; it is the field's DEFAULT.


================================================================================
BOTTOM LINE
================================================================================
By the standards of HGCN (NeurIPS 2019), Ganea HNN (NeurIPS 2018), kappa-GCN
(ICML 2020), HNN++ (ICLR 2021), Liu HGNN (NeurIPS 2019), HVT (LayerNorm-in-
tangent-space), and the sphere line (Mettes hyperspherical prototypes / Davidson
hyperspherical VAE / geoopt), our architecture is a legitimate, conventional
instance of the accepted tangent-space-linearization + geodesic-decoder paradigm.
Being single-log/single-exp with a per-node base point, it sits at the CLEANEST
end of that paradigm, not the criticized end. The critics argue for going fully
intrinsic to gain expressiveness and avoid REPEATED round-trips; those arguments
motivate a possible alternative but do NOT invalidate a single-projection design,
and non-rotation-equivariance in the tangent space is normal practice.

NOTE: this settles VALIDITY (is it legitimate Riemannian DL? yes). It is separate
from the open PERFORMANCE question (is the geometry load-bearing here, vs a
normalized Euclidean / Method-A version? — see the coin evidence: E doesn't
cluster, cosine ~ geodesic, the non-geometric stateless head beats it). Both
answers stand independently.
